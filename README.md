## 1. CBAM Embedded Emissions Calculator (`cbam-calculator.xlsx`)

### What it does
Calculates embedded greenhouse gas emissions per CN-code shipment for the six CBAM-covered product categories:
- **Iron & steel** (CN 7201–7229, 7301–7326)
- **Aluminium** (CN 7601–7616)
- **Cement** (CN 2523)
- **Fertilisers** (CN 3102–3105)
- **Hydrogen** (CN 2804 10 00)
- **Electricity** (CN 2716)

### Methodology
- Follows **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1773** (transitional period methodology)
- Supports both **default values** (Tab 2: CBAM defaults by country/product) and **actual embedded emissions** (Tab 3: actual-data entry)
- Output: embedded emissions in tCO₂e per tonne of goods — ready to paste into the CBAM DECLARANT portal

### Tabs
| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| Instructions | Step-by-step guide, glossary of terms |
| CBAM Default Values | EU Commission published defaults by country + product code |
| Actual Emissions Entry | Input form for actual plant-level production data |
| Financial Exposure Calculator | Estimated CBAM certificate cost at current EU ETS price |
| Importer Communication Template | Pre-filled email template for your EU importer |

### Who is this for?
- Steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser, hydrogen exporters from **Pakistan, India, China, Turkey, Vietnam, GCC, and North Africa**
- EU importers needing supplier data to complete quarterly CBAM reports
- Sustainability consultants building client CBAM data packages

> ⚠️ Note: CBAM does **not** currently cover textiles. If you're a textile mill, see our [EU Carbon Compliance guide for Pakistani Textile Exporters](https://sustainability-frisk.com) — CSRD Scope 3 buyer demands create an equivalent obligation.

## 2. EUDR Due Diligence Checklist (`eudr-checklist.docx` + `.pdf`)

### What it does
Step-by-step operator due diligence checklist for the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR), covering all 8 regulated commodities:
- Timber · Soy · Palm oil · Cattle/beef · Cocoa · Coffee · Rubber · Leather

### Contents
- **Section 1**: Information requirements (product description, geolocation of origin, quantity, supplier chain)
- **Section 2**: GPS polygon data collection guide (plots > 4 ha require individual polygons; < 4 ha require centroid coordinates)
- **Section 3**: Risk assessment matrix (country risk profile, satellite deforestation check, supplier due diligence)
- **Section 4**: Risk mitigation actions (audit protocols, corrective action templates)
- **Section 5**: DDS filing checklist (TRACES NT upload requirements)

### Regulatory reference
EUDR Regulation (EU) 2023/1115, including **Delegated Regulations** on country benchmarking and **Implementing Regulations** on the information system.

## 3. CSRD Scope 3 Data Collection Template (`csrd-scope3-template.xlsx`)

### What it does
Supplier-facing data request template covering all **15 GHG Protocol Scope 3 categories**, pre-mapped to **ESRS E1** climate disclosure requirements.

### Tabs
| Tab | Content |
|---|---|
| Cover sheet | Instructions for supplier completion, definitions |
| Cat 1 — Purchased Goods | Spend-based + average-data method inputs |
| Cat 3 — Fuel & Energy | Upstream transmission & distribution losses |
| Cat 4 — Upstream Transport | Weight × distance entry, default EFs |
| Cat 11 — Use of Sold Products | Active use phase EF entry |
| Cat 12 — EoL Treatment | Disposal route + weight entry |
| Emission Factor Reference | 200+ EFs for textiles, electronics, leather, food, steel |
| Output Summary | Auto-calculated tCO₂e per category for ESRS E1 table |

### Industries covered
Pre-loaded emission factors for: **Textile & apparel · Electronics & semiconductor · Leather goods · Food & beverage · Steel & aluminium · Packaging**

## 4. Supplier Carbon Questionnaire (`supplier-carbon-questionnaire.docx`)

Ready-to-send questionnaire for your tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers. Covers:
- Company profile + boundary definition
- Scope 1 energy sources (fuel, process emissions)
- Scope 2 electricity (market-based and location-based)
- Key Scope 3 categories by industry
- Third-party verification status
- Carbon reduction targets (SBTi / own targets)

Formatted for direct email dispatch. Available in **English** (and Urdu translation on request — email info@sustainability-frisk.com).
