# Supplier Carbon Data Questionnaire
## For CSRD Scope 3 Category 1 — Purchased Goods & Services

> **Published by**: [Sustainability-Frisk](https://sustainability-frisk.com) | MIT Licence  
> **Purpose**: Send to tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers to collect verified carbon data for your CSRD ESRS E1 disclosure  
> **Standard**: GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard + ESRS E1

---

**HOW TO USE THIS QUESTIONNAIRE**

1. Copy the template below into an email or Word document
2. Send to each tier-1 supplier covering >1% of your annual spend
3. Request completion within 30 days
4. Completed responses feed directly into the CSRD Scope 3 template

---

## GUIDANCE NOTES FOR SUPPLIERS

**Why is this data needed?**
Under the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), companies that sell to EU buyers must report their Scope 3 Category 1 emissions (purchased goods and services). Your energy and product carbon data is the most accurate input available. Without it, we must use database estimates which may overstate your products' footprint.

**What happens to the data?**
Your data is used only for our internal GHG inventory and CSRD regulatory disclosure. It will not be shared with competitors or used for commercial purposes beyond compliance reporting.

**Data quality note:**
ESRS E1 (the CSRD climate standard) requires us to disclose what percentage of Scope 3 data comes from supplier-specific data vs database estimates. Supplier-provided data (this questionnaire) is classified as "primary data" — the highest quality level. Database estimates are classified as secondary data.
