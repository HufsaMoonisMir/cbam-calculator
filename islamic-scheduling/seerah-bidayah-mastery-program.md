**Al-Bidāyah wa'l-Nihāyah (Ibn Kathīr)**
- Arabic: any critically edited multi-volume printing (e.g. Dār Hijr or Dār al-Fikr editions are commonly cited in secondary literature) — `[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]` for which specific critical edition you hold.
- English: multi-volume translations exist under titles such as *The Beginning and the End* / *Al-Bidayah wa'l-Nihayah* — `[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]` for translator, publisher, and volume-to-Arabic-volume mapping before using page citations.
- Urdu: Urdu translations exist from several Karachi/Lahore/Deoband-tradition presses — `[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]` for a specific in-print edition.

**Core Seerah sources**
- Ibn Hishām, *al-Sīra al-Nabawiyya* — English: A. Guillaume, *The Life of Muhammad* (Oxford). Urdu translations widely available — `[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]` for specific press.
- Ibn Isḥāq via Ibn Hishām's recension (no independent manuscript of Ibn Isḥāq survives complete) — used only through Ibn Hishām/al-Ṭabarī quotations.
- al-Wāqidī, *Kitāb al-Maghāzī* — English: trans. Rizwi Faizer et al. — flagged throughout the program per classical criticism of al-Wāqidī's reliability (many hadith scholars graded him weak in ḥadīth transmission even where his historical detail is used cautiously by historians).
- Ibn Saʿd, *al-Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā*.
- al-Ṭabarī, *Tārīkh al-Rusul wa'l-Mulūk* — English: SUNY Press *The History of al-Ṭabarī* series (multi-translator, relevant volumes for Seerah and Rāshidūn eras).
- al-Balādhurī, *Futūḥ al-Buldān* and *Ansāb al-Ashrāf*.
- al-Bayhaqī and Abū Nuʿaym, *Dalā'il al-Nubuwwah*.
- al-Qāḍī ʿIyāḍ, *al-Shifā*.
- Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah, *Zād al-Maʿād* — English trans. Jalal Abualrub (among others) — `[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]` for edition.
- Ṣafī al-Raḥmān al-Mubārakpūrī, *al-Raḥīq al-Makhtūm* (*The Sealed Nectar*) — useful synthesis, not a primary source; treat as secondary throughout.
- Martin Lings, *Muhammad: His Life Based on the Earliest Sources* — secondary synthesis in English, useful for narrative flow, not a citation source for grading reports.

**Hadith collections** — Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī, Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, Muwaṭṭa' Mālik, Sunan Abī Dāwūd, Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī, Sunan al-Nasā'ī, Sunan Ibn Mājah, Musnad Aḥmad — any standard numbered edition (e.g. the widely used Dār al-Salām numbering for the Six Books) is acceptable as long as the numbering system is stated consistently across the program.

## 4. Placement Assessment (30 questions — Track A and Track B kept separate)

Answer from existing knowledge; this calibrates the starting lesson, not a pass/fail gate. No sources needed — if unsure, write "unsure" rather than guessing.

### Track A — Seerah (15 questions)
1. What was the Prophet ﷺ's tribe, and what was Quraysh's role in Makkah before Islam?
2. Roughly what year is given for the Prophet ﷺ's birth, and what event traditionally is said to have occurred that year?
3. Who were the Prophet ﷺ's parents, and what happened to each during his childhood?
4. Who was Khadījah (رضي الله عنها), and what was her relationship to the Prophet ﷺ before marriage?
5. Where and how did the first revelation take place, according to the standard account?
6. Name two of the earliest converts to Islam.
7. What was the Boycott of Banū Hāshim, and roughly how long is it said to have lasted?
8. What is the Isrā' and Miʿrāj, and what obligation is traditionally linked to it?
9. What were the two 'Aqabah Pledges, and who were they made with?
10. In what year did the Hijrah to Madīnah take place, and what does "Hijrah" mean?
11. Name the three major battles between the Muslims and Quraysh in the Madīnan period, in order.
12. What was the Treaty of Ḥudaybiyyah, and why is it considered significant despite appearing unfavorable at the time?
13. What happened at the Conquest of Makkah, and how did the Prophet ﷺ treat the Quraysh who had persecuted him?
14. What was the Farewell Pilgrimage, and what is one thing said in the Farewell Sermon?
15. In what year did the Prophet ﷺ pass away, and who succeeded him as leader of the community?

### Track B — Al-Bidāyah wa'l-Nihāyah (15 questions)
1. Who is Ibn Kathīr, and roughly in what century did he write?
2. What does "Isrā'īliyyāt" refer to, and why does it require caution in Islamic history writing?
3. Name two of the prophets mentioned in the Qur'an prior to Ibrāhīm.
4. What is the basic outline of the story of Nūḥ (Noah) and the Flood as found in the Qur'an?
5. What is Ibrāhīm's traditional connection to the founding of Makkah and the Kaʿbah?
6. Summarize the basic outline of the story of Yūsuf (Joseph) in Egypt.
7. What is the basic outline of Mūsā (Moses) and Banū Isrā'īl's exodus from Egypt?
8. Name two prophets who came after Mūsā and before ʿĪsā.
9. What do you know about ʿĪsā (Jesus) in the Islamic tradition, and how does it differ from mainstream Christian belief on his end?
10. What is the "Fatrah," and why is it significant for the Seerah?
11. Who were the two major world empires bordering Arabia at the start of the 7th century CE?
12. Name the four Rightly-Guided Caliphs (al-Khulafā' al-Rāshidūn) in order.
13. What is the Ridda (Apostasy) Wars, and under which caliph did they occur?
14. What happened to ʿUthmān, and how did it lead to the first civil war (Fitna) in Islamic history?
15. What is al-Fitan wa'l-Malāḥim, and what kind of material does it generally cover?
